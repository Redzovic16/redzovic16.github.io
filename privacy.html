01Scope

This policy applies to the SON ANDA Android game developed by Redzovic and this support website. The current app does not offer an online player account, a developer-operated game-data server, or active real-money purchases. This policy will be updated if these features change.

02Game information on your device

Your gold and diamond balances, cosmetic ownership, records, calendar and mission progress, chests, sound settings and game preferences are stored in the app's local device storage. The current version does not send this game information to the developer's server. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data can permanently remove your local progress.

03Age ranges and children

An age range, rather than a full date of birth, is selected to apply age-appropriate advertising and privacy settings. The selection is stored on your device. The app does not send ad-loading or ad-display requests for users aged 12 or younger, or when no age range has been selected. These users can play and receive rewards that do not require ads. Protective ad settings apply to users aged 13–17.

04Advertising and Google services

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs. When the advertising service is used, Google may process information such as IP addresses, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics under its own terms and policies. Advertising ID and AdServices advertising-ID, topics and attribution permissions have been removed from the app; this does not mean that no other technical information is processed. A G ad-content rating limit applies to all ad requests.

05Your advertising and privacy choices

Where required, choices are collected through a Google UMP message. The app does not load ads unless the SDK allows ad requests. Where applicable, advertising privacy choices can be reopened from the diamond workshop. Rewarded ads are optional: you do not need to watch a rewarded ad to start playing or begin a free new run. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for details.

06Sharing and device permissions

The share button opens Android's share screen only when you choose it. You decide which app or recipient receives the message. SON ANDA does not access your contacts for this purpose. The current version does not track a friend's installation or real referral rewards on a server. Camera, microphone and precise-location permissions are not requested.

07Purchases

Real in-app purchases are not active in the current version. The app does not request card details or collect payment. Purchase verification and related data processing will be added to this policy before purchasing is enabled.

08This website

The developer has not added analytics tools, advertisements or tracking cookies to this website. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical records such as visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External websites have their own policies.

09Retention, deletion and contact

You can delete local game data from Android's app settings; this also removes game progress. Data processed by Google or GitHub is subject to those services' retention and privacy rules. Send privacy questions or requests to onurbuyukdere@gmail.com. This page will be updated when the contact address or data-processing features change.
